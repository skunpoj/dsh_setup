Security by OWASP: the DSH controls grouped by the ten agentic risks

The OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications is the primary grouping of the 80 DSH features. Each feature also carries its DSH risk, its enforcement layer and its confidentiality/integrity/availability property.

Reading note. Evidence status comes from the full comparison: 63 features are "Designed but not verified", 14 are "Future roadmap", 3 have "A2A source reviewed" status. A tag in this view is a draft grouping for review; it is not an OWASP certification, and no claim of OWASP compliance is made.

Design principle: flag, pause, human in the loop. When a request, a result or an agent action is suspicious or exceeds policy, the platform flags it and pauses it for a person to decide; no AI agent decides or signs off on its own control. Acceptance tests for goal hijack (ASI01), tool misuse (ASI02), inter-agent communication (ASI07) and human-agent trust (ASI09) are run and signed off by named human owners; an AI session may prepare test scripts but does not accept results. (Owner instruction of 10 Nov 2026; the same pattern as the approval gate in the DQ safety layers.)

One request through the layers

The harness and the infrastructure enforce; model instructions do not. Every stop point returns a denial, a hint or a fallback to the agent, never a silent success.
