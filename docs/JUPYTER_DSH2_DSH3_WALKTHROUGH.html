Open Jupyter in DSH2 and DSH3

A silent, captioned instructional illustration of owner sign-in, startup when needed, existing-kernel reuse and administrator controls.

  1. Sign in to your own workspace. Use the designated owner account for DSH2 or DSH3. These two instances use their assigned Internet environment.
  2. Open JupyterLab. Choose JupyterLab at sign-in or open its link from the workspace. Startup uses your authenticated session.
  3. Keep existing work running. An available Jupyter server is reused. Opening Jupyter does not restart its kernels or erase their variables.
  4. Start only when needed. If Jupyter is stopped, the Starting Jupyter page requests a controlled launch and opens the notebook interface when ready.
  5. Respect administrator controls. A disabled Jupyter service cannot be started by the user. Opening Jupyter does not change access approvals or service settings.
  6. Save notebooks and settings. Saved notebooks, workspace files, settings and DSH history remain durable. A full workspace restart ends running kernels and in-memory variables.
