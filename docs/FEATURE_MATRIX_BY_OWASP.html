The 80 DSH features, grouped by their primary OWASP agentic risk, with the DSH risk, the enforcement layer, the confidentiality/integrity/availability property and the control type.

Recorded primary OWASP groupings retained; current operational acceptance remains separate. The status of every feature is shown in its evidence column; see the evidence status legend. Current reference alignment: original on-prem architecture draft and two foundation diagrams; private workbook J/K/L control layers and M cross-layer audit. The workbook payload stays private. Leakage, damage and result containment are risk objectives, separate from the policy/human, harness/deterministic and infrastructure/resource enforcement layers. The 76 primary-category rows plus four ALL rows retain every stable feature ID, original runtime comparison and evidence state.

Ten OWASP categories

Taxonomy: OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications 2026. Primary assignments count each of the 76 control or roadmap rows once. Four cross-cutting evidence rows apply to all ten categories. Secondary mappings overlap and are not additional features. A category assigns a feature to an OWASP risk; the evidence column says whether its control is verified.

How to read the comparison columns. The first four columns list the feature rows by number. The next four compare, for each OWASP category, the multiharness (our gateway, controllers and sandbox) with each of the DSH Python SDK, Claude Code and Deep Agents used alone, outside the multiharness. The standalone cells restate what the full comparison says the deployer must supply; they say nothing about what the product can or cannot do. The last column adds the on-prem AI analysis architecture draft (On_prem_AI_analysis_architecture_draft.pptx, 10 slides, sha256 27cd3a8a0008da8186b1e8018872291cad164a6586045e8ff935a1385ca335db): a design draft marked for review and not deployed, shown as generic control principles only. No cell is an OWASP certification or a verified result.
